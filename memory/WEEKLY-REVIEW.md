- **When the pre-buy gate blocks pre-positioning on tier-1 binary + weekend-tail grounds, the bar is doing its job — but when three-of-four sessions in a week carry that blocker, the "act on any A-grade thesis" meta-rule becomes structurally unreachable inside the routine cadence.** The strategy's operational bind this week: pre-market → market-open runs pre-print; midday runs 12:30 ET. The corresponding lesson is that the strategy will produce zero-open weeks in tier-1-heavy calendars almost by construction, and the cumulative SPX gap over such windows is a floor cost of the cadence, not a bar-too-tight failure.
